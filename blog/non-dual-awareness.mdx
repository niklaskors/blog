## Non-dual awareness

Readings on metaphysical idealism led me into a new stream of knowledge that had previously been unknown to me. For me,
this other way of knowing perfectly captures the more intuitive aspects of the same ideas and therefore aligns closely
with idealism. The perspective I'm referring to is that of spiritualism and its related practices. The person who
articulates these ideas in the present day—and the author of the book I’ve begun reading—is
[Rupert Spira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Spira). He explores the specific spiritual tradition of nonduality,
whose central metaphysical claim is that everything is fundamentally reducible to a single, indivisible reality—what we
might call pure consciousness or awareness. For those familiar with idealism, the resemblance between their conclusions
and presuppositions becomes apparent. I have always had an interest in the eastern traditions and religions, with
Buddhism in particular. This is most likely due to the gains in popularity of mindfulness, yoga and meditation in the
west. Now, this topic was reintroduced to me and described out in the book "You Are the Happiness You Seek". In this
article I have outlined some of the things that stuck with me while reading this book.

### Experience

If fundamentally, everything can be reduced to awareness, we cannot claim (or verify) that anything is able to exist
outside of it. Therefore, things can only exist within the scope of awareness. The name for things that exist or take
place within the scope of awareness can be described as experiences.

### Quotes

"I began to feel what I had often read about in the traditional literature but had never been able to verify for myself:
namely, that awareness extends beyond the limits of the body and the finite mind within which it seems to be housed, and
encompasses the entire universe."
